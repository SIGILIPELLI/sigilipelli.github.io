Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy applies to services sold by Learnmasterypath (Mastery Path) on learnmasterypath.com. Our paid Resume Tools are digital services delivered instantly on screen after payment.

Cancellation

You can cancel at any time before completing payment by closing the payment window; you will not be charged. Because each service is generated and delivered immediately after payment, an order cannot be cancelled once the result has been delivered.

When you are entitled to a refund

  • Result not delivered: if your payment succeeded but no result was generated (for example a technical error), first use the "Retry with the same payment" button on the page. If it still fails, we refund the full amount.
  • Charged twice: duplicate payments for the same order are refunded in full.
  • Unsatisfactory result: if the result is clearly unusable (for example blank, in the wrong language, or unrelated to your resume), contact us within 7 days of purchase and we will either regenerate it or refund you.

How to request a refund

Email contact@learnmasterypath.com within 7 days of purchase with your Razorpay payment ID (shown on the page after payment and in your payment receipt) and a short description of the problem. We reply within 2 working days.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method through Razorpay within 5–7 working days. The time for the money to appear depends on your bank or UPI provider.
