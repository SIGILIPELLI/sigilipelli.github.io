Delivery Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Mastery Path sells digital services only. Nothing is shipped physically and there are no shipping charges.

How delivery works

  • After your payment succeeds, your result is generated and shown on the same page, usually within one minute.
  • Tailored resumes and cover letters can be downloaded as Word documents or saved as PDF, and copied as text.
  • Your latest result is also kept in your own browser so you can reopen the page and download it again.

If delivery fails

If the page closes or an error occurs after payment, reopen learnmasterypath.com/resume in the same browser and use "Get my result" or "Retry with the same payment". If you still don't receive your result, email contact@learnmasterypath.com with your payment ID and we will deliver it or refund you, as described in our refund policy.

Service area

The services are available online to customers in India paying in Indian Rupees.
