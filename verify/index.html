Verify a certificate

Enter the certificate ID printed at the bottom of a Mastery Path certificate to see who earned it, for which course, with what score and when.

What a Mastery Path certificate shows

  • The learner passed the course's certification exam: 25 questions drawn at random from all four levels, with a 40-minute limit and a 70% pass mark.
  • The exam is set and marked on our server, and each certificate is recorded here when it is issued.
  • It is an online, unproctored exam: we do not check identity.
  • It is a platform certificate from Mastery Path, not an accredited academic or professional qualification.

Earn one free: open any course and go to its Final Quiz page. Questions? Contact us.
