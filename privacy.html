Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

This page covers learnmasterypath.com (the Mastery Path hub) and, by the same terms, every individual course site linked from it (learnmasterypath.com/<course>-mastery-path/).

Cookies & data collection

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices.

This site also uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and usage patterns (which pages are visited, roughly how long, and general device/location info) — no personally identifying information is collected. You can opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Resume Tools (paid services)

When you use the Resume Tools, the free ATS scan runs entirely in your browser. For paid services, your resume and the job description are sent securely to our service and to our AI provider, Anthropic, only to generate your result. We do not store your resume or job text on our servers. Payments are processed by Razorpay, which collects your payment details under its own privacy policy; we receive only the payment ID, amount and status.

Email sign-ups (Interview Prep Pack)

If you enter your email address to get the free Interview Prep Pack, we store your email, the course page you signed up from, and the date, on Cloudflare's servers. We use it only to send occasional updates about new free courses and resources from Mastery Path. We never sell or share your email. You can unsubscribe from any email we send, or ask us to delete your address at any time by writing to contact@learnmasterypath.com.

Learning progress (stored in your browser)

Lesson ticks, quiz scores, notes and your saved name are kept in your own browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. They are not sent to us, and you can clear them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Certification exam and certificates

If you take a course's certification exam, your answers are sent to our service (on Cloudflare) to be marked. An unfinished exam is deleted after three hours. If you pass, we store the name you entered, the course, your score and the date under a certificate ID, so that anyone you give the ID to can check the certificate at learnmasterypath.com/verify. We keep your network address for up to an hour only to limit how many exams can be started, and we do not link it to the certificate. Certificate records are kept until you ask us to delete one; write to contact@learnmasterypath.com with the certificate ID.

What this site does not do

No account is required anywhere in this network. Apart from the optional email sign-up, the optional certification exam and the resume text you choose to submit for a paid service, all described above, no forms collect personal information. Nothing here is sold to third parties beyond the standard operation of the AdSense advertising network described above. Every page — course content, this policy, the license page — is publicly viewable with no login, signup, or paywall.

Contact

Questions can be sent to contact@learnmasterypath.com.
