About Mastery Path

A free, structured learning library

Mission

Make structured, practical technology and professional education free for anyone, in a clear order from first steps to expert level, without accounts, paywalls or upsells inside the lessons.

What Mastery Path is

A library of 102 courses (93 technology, 9 professional development) with 3,672 lessons, 306 projects and 102 capstones across 15 categories. Every course is free to read with no sign-up. Numbers on this site come from the course registry and are re-measured from the live site; see how we count.

Ownership and authorship

Mastery Path is owned, directed and maintained by Vinod Kumar Sigilipelli, who chooses the courses, sets their structure and publishes them. The lessons and quizzes are written with the help of AI (Claude, by Anthropic), and code examples and quiz answers are checked by running them wherever the technology can be run. Where a quiz or challenge uses a simplified model instead of the real product, it says so. The platform has no investors, sponsors or partner institutions. Course source files are public on GitHub, so every lesson's history is open to inspection.

Learning method

Every course follows the same four-level path, so you always know where you are and what comes next:

  • Level 1 – Entry: setup, core concepts and the vocabulary of the subject, with guided examples.
  • Level 2 – Intermediate: the everyday techniques you need to be productive, applied to realistic tasks.
  • Level 3 – Advanced: deeper patterns, performance, troubleshooting and real-world trade-offs.
  • Level 4 – Master: professional practice, end-to-end design and a final capstone project.

Lessons include runnable or practical examples and an exercise; each level ends with a project; module quizzes give instant feedback; and the final certification exam is marked on our server, with certificates that anyone can verify. Certificates are platform certificates, not accredited qualifications.

How courses are developed and reviewed

  1. A syllabus is planned in four levels with outcomes for each level.
  2. Lessons are written in Markdown with examples that are run or checked before publishing.
  3. Changes are published from version-controlled repositories, so each update is dated.
  4. An automated audit checks every course against the published eligibility rules (levels, lesson length, reachable pages, duplicates).
  5. Human content review is the final step before a course counts toward any record measurement. Each review will be recorded with a date and reviewer; none have been recorded yet, and the quality page shows the current number. Learner corrections are welcome through the contact page.

History

  • July 2026: first courses published (earliest course commit: 17 July 2026); by 19 November 2026 the library reached 102 courses, each in four levels.
  • September 2026: brand, search and career tools (free ATS resume check) added.
  • November 2026: interactive learning layer, module quizzes, interview-question pages, the certification exam with verifiable certificates, the course registry and public audit.

How it stays free

Mastery Path is supported by advertising through Google AdSense and by optional paid resume services. Ads help pay for writing and maintaining the courses. See the privacy policy for details.
