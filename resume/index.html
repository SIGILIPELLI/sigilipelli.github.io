Questions

Does it make things up?

No. The AI is instructed to use only facts from your resume. It rewords, reorders and emphasizes what you already did to match the job, and lists requirements you don't yet show so you can address them honestly.

How fast is it?

Results appear on this page within about a minute of payment. Keep this page open until your result shows.

What if something goes wrong after I pay?

If the result fails to generate, you can retry with the same payment, or use "Already paid? Get your result again" with your payment ID. If it still fails, email contact@learnmasterypath.com with your payment ID and we will refund you.

Do you store my resume?

No. Your resume and the job post are used only to produce your result and are not saved on our servers. Your latest result is kept only in your own browser.

Do I need an account?

No account and no subscription. You pay once for each result.
